    How to hold your cube

    1. Starting position: green center facing you, white center on top (so red is on your right). Centers never move, so this always works.
    2. F — Front: enter the green face exactly as you see it, top-left sticker first.
    3. R, B, L: turn the whole cube a quarter turn to the left so red faces you (white still on top) and enter R. Keep turning left for blue (B) and orange (L). Then return to green.
    4. U — Up: from the starting position, tip the top toward you so white faces you. Green is now at the bottom of the grid and blue at the top.
    5. D — Down: from the starting position, tip the bottom up toward you so yellow faces you. Green is now at the top of the grid.
    6. Double-check: the colored bars around every grid show which neighboring center touches that edge.

    Move notation

    Each letter is a face. Look straight at that face to judge the direction.

    R'“R prime”: 90° counter-clockwise
